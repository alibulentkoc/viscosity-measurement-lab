Viscosity Measurement Laboratory — Grading Tool

Instructor use · measurement audit and dual answer computation

Enter the master measurements for the bench once, then a group's reported values. Each reading is checked against the master within a tolerance you can edit. Every answer is then computed twice: from the master, which is the correct answer for this bench, and from what the group actually wrote down, which is what their arithmetic should have produced. Grade the arithmetic against the second column and the measuring against the first.
