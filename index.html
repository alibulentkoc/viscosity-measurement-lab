Viscosity Measurement Laboratory

Fluid Power Systems & Internal Combustion Engines

Three instruments measure the same property by different routes, and the three routes do not always agree. Explaining the disagreement is the point of the laboratory. Everything below runs in the browser with no network access.

students
Interactive Laboratory
Falling ball, Saybolt and rotary viscometers, unit conversion, temperature analysis, notebook and export.
 students
Student Handout
Printable procedure, data tables and analysis questions. Print to PDF from any browser.
 instructors
Answer Sheet
Enter the bench master measurements and every answer recomputes, with substituted working and model answers.
 instructors
Grading Tool
Audits reported readings against tolerances and computes every answer twice, so measurement error and arithmetic error grade apart.
